Getting the responses out of Google Forms
  1. Open the form and go to the Responses tab.
  2. Click Link to Sheets to create a responses spreadsheet.
  3. In the sheet, choose File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv), then upload it above.

2Matching rules

Pairs score highest when both people picked the same top interest, then by overlap in the career paths they checked. Every junior gets one new member before anyone gets a second.
